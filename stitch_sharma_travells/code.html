About Us

Welcome to Sharma Travel Agents

Welcome to Sharma Travel Agents, a proud subsidiary of Aggarwal Travels – your trusted partner for safe, comfortable, and affordable travel across India. Based in Delhi, we specialize in offering a wide range of vehicles on rent, tailored to every travel need, from family trips and group outings to weddings, corporate tours, and airport transfers.

Our fleet includes 4+1 seater sedans, 6/7 seater MUVs, 12 to 16 seater Tempo Travellers, 20 to 26 seater Tempo Travellers, 12 to 16 seater Urbania vehicles, 27 to 29 seater mini buses, and 45 to 66 seater luxury buses.

At Sharma Travel Agents, we prioritize convenience and transparency. Booking your ride is simple via call or message, and you can pay using cash, card, or online transfer. Our professional drivers ensure your journey is safe, timely, and enjoyable.
